About AgriTechNiks

AgriTechNiks is an innovative manufacturing company founded by twelve senior high school students passionate about advancing agriculture. Based in Valley Golf, San Juan, Cainta, Rizal, our mission is to advocate for sustainability by reducing, reusing, and recycling organic waste materials.

Our flagship product, a cutting-edge soil cultivator machine, combines soil and organic waste to produce fertile soil, fostering faster plant growth and promoting eco-friendly agricultural practices. Designed for hobbyists, gardeners, and agricultural enthusiasts, our solution helps households take an active role in environmental conservation.

At AgriTechNiks, we are committed to delivering high-quality products that enhance our customers' planting experiences while making a positive impact on the environment.
