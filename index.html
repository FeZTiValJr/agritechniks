About AgriTechNiks

AgriTechniks is a manufacturing company with twelve senior high school students that joined together to establish soil cultivator machine that can be helpful in agriculture based on our previous research study. The company is in Valley Golf San Juan Cainta, Rizal. We vision to advocate the goal of sustainability to promote reducing, reusing, and recycling organic waste materials by utilizing a machine that can process combining the soil and organic waste to produce fertile soil for faster plant growth in the community. The mission we want to accomplish is to bring experience to our customers with the aim of achieving their satisfaction. This is designed for those who do planting as a hobby and recreational activity. The company wants the consumer to encourage reducing their own waste to embark helping the environment starts within houses..
